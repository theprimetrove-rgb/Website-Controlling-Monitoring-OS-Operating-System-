Frequently asked questions

Important questions before starting a project.

Can RestaurantOS work for several branches?

Yes. The Professional and Enterprise editions can organise orders, reservations, customers, menus and table information by branch.

Can the website design be customised?

Yes. Brand colours, typography, imagery, homepage structure, menu presentation and customer journey can be adapted. Extensive custom design is included in the Enterprise scope.

Are hosting, domain and payment-gateway charges included?

No. The package prices cover design and development of the agreed platform. Hosting, domain registration, payment-gateway fees and paid third-party services are billed separately.

Can online payments be added?

Yes. A suitable payment gateway can be integrated as a separately scoped feature, subject to the provider’s technical requirements and commercial approval.

Is there a monthly software fee?

The rates shown are one-time development prices. Ongoing hosting, maintenance, support or third-party subscriptions can be arranged separately if required.

Can it later support clothing, retail or service businesses?

The underlying design approach is modular, but each industry version requires its own customised workflows, terminology and interface. It is not presented as a ready-made universal product.
